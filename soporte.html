Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?

The app is free to download. The full course is unlocked with a one-time payment of €9.99. No subscriptions, no recurring charges: pay once and it is yours forever.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once downloaded, the app and all audio work without internet — on a plane, the metro, wherever you like.

The microphone does not recognise my voice well

Grant microphone permission (iPhone Settings → Italiano in 30 → Microphone) and speak somewhere without much noise. If you prefer, you can type the answer instead of speaking.

Can I continue my progress on another device?

Yes, by turning on syncing (optional) in the app settings.

How do I delete my data?

From Settings → Delete my data inside the app. Everything is deleted completely.
